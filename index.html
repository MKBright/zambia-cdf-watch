Why this demo exists

  • Coverage of the Auditor General’s 2024 report says about 71,000 school desks, worth about K130 million, were paid for but not delivered. Zambia Monitor
  • An IGC policy brief reported that only 27% of 2024 CDF projects were complete by the first quarter of 2025. Chomba and co-authors, July 2025
  • Transparency International Zambia has asked for project status to be published more clearly. TI-Z statement

The cards, map, and table below do not measure those findings. Every project is invented so the page can demonstrate a tracker without pretending to be an official record.
